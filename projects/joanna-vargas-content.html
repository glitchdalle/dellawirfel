typeperformance analysis
year2025–26
toolsexcel
rolecontent strategist

summary

what drives reach vs. engagement across instagram and tiktok.

overview

an analysis of published content across formats (text overlay, talking, voiceover) and content pillars, with performance tracked across instagram and tiktok.

main image

observations

text overlay drove the highest reach (10k–12k+ views); talking and voiceover were more moderate and inconsistent. posts with similar reach varied widely in likes, saves and shares, and instagram outperformed tiktok on most content.

outliers

three tiktok outliers made the point: a 96k-view post drove strong engagement and 231 follows, an 84k-view post drew almost no interaction, and a 41k-view post converted strongly for its size. high reach does not guarantee engagement.

recommendations

prioritize formats that drive engagement and follower growth, not just reach; replicate the traits of high performers (topic relevance, format, pacing); and test voiceover + text overlay for more consistent results.
