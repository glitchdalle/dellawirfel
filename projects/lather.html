summary

packaging for a bodycare line rooted in uzbek home remedies.

concept

lather is a bodycare line built on ingredients commonly used in uzbekistan: greek yogurt in the hair mask for its lactic acid, an olive-oil-enriched body butter, and a lash serum made with the taramira plant. the packaging puts a twist on traditional tajik-uzbek floral prints, paired with hand-drawn characters.
