summary

an app-style wellbeing visualization platform. my m.s. capstone thesis, in progress.

overview

an interactive, iphone-sized platform that turns daily self-reports into reflective, aesthetically considered visualizations. it isn't a fitness tracker or a medical dashboard. it's about personal informatics: helping people notice their own patterns without being judged, scored or told what to fix.

main image

daily check-in

a short, gentle check-in records four subjective measures on a 1–5 scale (energy, overwhelm, calm and social exhaustion) alongside context: date and time, social context (solitary / some social / very social), location (home / mixed / mostly out) and outdoor exposure. over time these accumulate into a personal dataset.

analysis

temporal views show how each measure moves across days and weeks. contextual views explore associations, like energy and outdoor time, or overwhelm and social context, always framed as observed associations, never causes. exploratory clustering looks for recurring configurations of days: patterns of experience, not diagnoses.

visual direction

atmospheric, minimal and contemplative, with geometric and organic forms, controlled gradients and editorial layouts instead of generic healthcare blues and dashboard grids. every visual choice has to encode something real: beauty with a defensible analytical meaning.

principles

reflection over optimization. exploration over prescription. analytical integrity that shows uncertainty. gentle interaction that respects attention. a central research question is how to support real self-understanding without encouraging over-monitoring, misreading or anxiety.

status

in progress: capstone for the m.s. in data analysis & visualization at the cuny graduate center, expected january 2027.
