NYC 311: housing & health

typeinteractive dashboard
year2025
toolstableau, NYC open data
roleanalyst & designer

summary

where mold, pest and heating complaints concentrate, 2019–2023.

question

which NYC neighborhoods reported the most complaints about mold, pests and heating outages between 2019 and 2023, and what does that reveal about housing quality and health risk? these aren't just maintenance problems; they affect health, especially in communities already facing poverty and aging housing.

four layers, one city

each circle is a zip code, sized by its 311 complaints from 2019 to 2023. scroll to stack the four complaint types on top of each other, one layer at a time.

    from the MAP-ALL and bar chart sheets of my tableau workbook. each layer is sized against its own busiest zip, so rarer complaints like mold stay visible. 12 complaints filed under zip 12345, which maps outside the city, aren't shown. borough totals come from the bar chart and include complaints with no zip.

    interactive — hover and click to explore

    data

    NYC 311 service requests from NYC open data, filtered to mold, pest and heat/hot-water complaints, using complaint type, borough, zip, date and status. because 311 depends on people reporting, some neighborhoods may be undercounted.

    findings

    the bronx and brooklyn had the most complaints, concentrated in the south bronx and central brooklyn, areas that often overlap with older housing and lower incomes. heating complaints spike in winter; mold and pests rise in summer. the map, timeline and borough comparison point toward targeted inspections and stronger tenant protections.
