roleanalyst & designer

summary

what a week of phone data describes, and what it leaves out.

question

what does a week of phone data actually describe, and what does it leave out? rather than treating screen time as a problem to optimize, this project looks at how counting attention turns ordinary habits into something that feels measurable and meaningful.

interactive — hover and click to explore

data

seven consecutive days in november, reconstructed from apple's screen time: total minutes, pickups, notifications, most-used app and social media time, with work, class and commute hours added as context.

visualization

stacked bars show daily screen time; a smaller chart shows physical activity, compressed and sparse by comparison; a line chart tracks pickups and notifications, which stay steady even on lighter days. the habit of checking reads as routine, not deliberate.

takeaway

the week reads as dense and uneven, with the phone as a constant background presence structuring attention. the short timeframe and apple's own tracking logic are limits the project treats as part of the story.
