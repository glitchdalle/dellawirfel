hello

I'm della, a designer who works with data. I like to tell stories with data and design, and I'd rather show an idea than describe it: a screen you can tap or a chart you can scroll says more than a paragraph about it.

why doordash

after covid, I would dash whenever I came home from college. I learned so much more about my community, got to know a lot of new streets, and caught a flash of everyone's life going on besides mine.

how I work

this site is an example. the NYC 311 map went from spikes to round lines, gained pinch-to-zoom on mobile, and ended on one shared scale so every line compares honestly. I'm a bit detail-obsessed, so every number on it is checked against its source.
